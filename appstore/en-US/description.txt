Prohisob is construction and renovation bookkeeping for foremen. Every sum of every project in one place: how much the client paid, how much went to workers and suppliers, who is still owed money, and how much cash is on hand.

The app interface is in Uzbek.

KEY FEATURES
• Projects: client payments, real expenses, cash on hand, progress against the contract
• Workers: earned, paid, outstanding debt — per worker and per project
• Suppliers: materials taken, paid, debt (including purchases on credit)
• Add a record in 10 seconds: amount, project, worker, note and a receipt photo from the camera
• Receipts: photos and PDFs attached to every record
• Reports by month and project, charts, Excel export
• Net profit for completed projects

FOR YOUR TEAM AND YOUR CLIENT
• Employees add records and can delete only their own
• The property owner signs in with their own login and sees only their project: what they paid, where the money went (with receipts) and the balance

SECURITY
• A separate workspace for every foreman
• Face ID app lock
• No ads, no tracking

The app works together with the cabinet.prohisob.com dashboard and the Telegram bot — the data is the same everywhere. An account is required: message us on Telegram @khamdamov and we will help you get started.
